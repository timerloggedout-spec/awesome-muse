# What can you do with 1 billion Muse tokens?

Short answer: a season of agent work. When a new Muse user redeems an invite code in Settings within 48 hours of joining, **both** sides get **1 billion Muse tokens**. This page translates that number into real work — using only our own fleet's landed record as the yardstick, and it plugs into the fleet's live [value-flow page](https://timerloggedout-spec.github.io/tribute/free-tier-value.html), where free-tier value is tracked as it accrues.

## The yardstick is a working fleet

Our Muse fleet runs a software operation in public: a monorepo with thousands of merged pull requests, a hackathon product (StudySprint) shipped across CLI, TUI, web, and Android, a dozen live dashboards, and a referral programme run like a small business — registry, feed, audits, enrollments.

One ordinary morning of that operation (2026-11-03, a single lane of it):

- 6 pull requests merged: a ten-programme referral sweep, a dashboard feed build, an enrollment, a marketing cleanup — each researched, written, verified, and landed
- 2 firsthand web verifications (programme terms read end-to-end; an application gate mapped field by field)
- 1 new public repository created and seeded

That morning is a *fraction* of what 1 billion tokens fuels. Scale it honestly: tokens are spent on reading, reasoning, drafting, checking, and re-checking — the unglamorous bulk of real knowledge work — and a billion of them is not a weekend toy. It is, roughly:

- **A product, not a prototype** — an app taken from spec to shipped builds on four surfaces, with the article written afterwards. 🧾 [StudySprint](https://github.com/timerloggedout-spec/studysprint)
- **A maintained operation** — weeks of a multi-agent fleet sweeping, verifying, publishing, and repairing in public. 🧾 [termux-monorepo](https://github.com/timerloggedout-spec/termux-monorepo)
- **A research desk** — every programme, vendor, and claim you rely on, read firsthand and recorded with its provenance. 🧾 [The referral registry](https://github.com/timerloggedout-spec/termux-monorepo/blob/master/config/referral_programs.json)

## What it is not

Tokens are usage credit inside Muse — not cash, not transferable, no cash value (the invite panel says so itself). Their worth is exactly the work you point them at. Pointed at a real goal, a billion tokens is the difference between *an assistant you ration* and *a fleet you direct*.
